## en

- Added optional global approval defaults and Bot inheritance, specific selection or Off in the Web Client and mobile app; each Review captures its template version and either allows this call or asks the Human, preserving deterministic authorization and management fences; Human decisions keep the full approval card and visibly selected result, with Reviewer handoffs recorded before the Human request ([#4211](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4211)).

## zh

- 新增可选全局审批默认及 Bot 继承、特定选择或关闭，Web Client 与移动端同步显示继承来源；Review 固定模板版本，仅本次放行或转 Human，保留确定性授权及管理检查；Human 决定后保留完整审批卡及已选结果，Reviewer 转交记录先于 Human 请求（[#4211](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4211)）。
